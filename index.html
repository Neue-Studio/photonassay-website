The Technology

Find more gold

For centuries, fire assay set the rhythm of gold mining. It worked but it destroyed the sample, relied on hazardous chemicals, poisoned workers and made its call on just 30 grams from a tonne of ore.

PhotonAssay™ rethinks the system. Using high-energy X-rays to excite atomic nuclei in geological samples, PhotonAssay™ delivers gold concentration data in approximately two minutes — with no chemicals, no lead-contaminated waste and no sample destruction.

The Process

From sample to result in minutes

01

Sample preparation

Geological samples are dried, crushed, sealed into unique QR-coded jars and placed on a conveyor belt. Automation does the rest.

02

X-ray analysis

The linear accelerator (LINAC) hits the sample with high-energy X-rays exciting the atomic nuclei before measuring the unique signature of the specific element.

03

Automated reporting

Results are delivered to LIMS within minutes and the sample remains intact, available for storage or remeasurement.

Sustainability

Better for people, business and the planet

Zero
Scope 1 emissions

PhotonAssay™ generates zero direct greenhouse gas emissions during analysis. Unlike fire assay, which requires combustion of lead-based fluxes at extreme temperatures, PhotonAssay™ is entirely electrically powered.

90%
Lower energy use

Approximately ten times less energy is consumed during a PhotonAssay™ analysis compared to traditional fire assay. The entire process is automated and requires no sustained high-temperature furnace operation.

Zero
Hazardous Waste

PhotonAssay™ completely eliminates lead-contaminated waste from the assay process. Samples remain intact and uncontaminated, available for storage or remeasurement.

PhotonAssay™ uses blanks and Certified Reference Materials to validate instrument performance, precision and calibration in real time. Every result is accompanied by an instrument precision value calculated specifically for that sample — not a batch average, but a value tied to exactly what was just measured. The system automatically flags samples with unusual properties, including high naturally occurring uranium or thorium, interfering elements, or unusually heterogeneous samples that may indicate coarse or nuggety gold. All QA/QC data is logged digitally and integrates seamlessly with LIMS and mine information systems, maintaining complete audit trails. Unit performance is benchmarked against the global PhotonAssay™ fleet and monitored by specialist support around the clock.
PhotonAssay™ has been validated against a broad range of Certified Reference Materials, showing excellent agreement with fire assay across all ore types and grades, with a correlation greater than 0.99. In most cases the same CRMs used in your current fire assay workflow are compatible with PhotonAssay™. For specific compatibility guidance, reach out to your local PhotonAssay™ laboratory or specialist.
A high-energy linear accelerator (LINAC) fires precisely timed pulses of X-rays through a 500-gram sample. Those X-rays penetrate the entire sample — not just the surface — exciting atomic nuclei directly. Gold, silver and copper each respond with a distinct gamma ray signature. Precision germanium detectors read those signatures and return a result in under two minutes. The whole process is fully automated. Samples are barcoded, queued, irradiated and measured without operator intervention. Results are delivered digitally to LIMS, with QA/QC data logged in real time.
