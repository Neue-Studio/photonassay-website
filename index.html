The Technology

A modern replacement for fire assay

For over a century, fire assay has been the standard for gold analysis. It's slow, toxic, and destructive. PhotonAssay™ changes everything.

Using high-energy X-rays to excite atomic nuclei in geological samples, PhotonAssay™ delivers gold concentration data in approximately two minutes — with no chemicals, no lead-contaminated waste, and no sample destruction.

The Process

From sample to result in minutes

01

Sample preparation

Geological samples are dried, crushed and loaded into standard sample trays. No fluxing or chemical preparation required.

02

X-ray analysis

The PhotonAssay™ unit bombards the sample with high-energy X-rays, exciting atomic nuclei and measuring their unique gamma-ray signatures.

03

Automated reporting

Results are processed and delivered automatically within minutes. The sample remains intact and available for further analysis.

Sustainability

Better for people, for
business and the planet

Zero
Scope 1 emissions

PhotonAssay™ generates zero direct greenhouse gas emissions during analysis. Unlike fire assay, which requires combustion of lead-based fluxes at extreme temperatures, PhotonAssay™ is entirely electrically powered.

90%
Lower energy use

Approximately ten times less energy is consumed during a PhotonAssay™ analysis compared to traditional fire assay. The entire process is automated and requires no sustained high-temperature furnace operation.

100%
Waste elimination

PhotonAssay™ completely eliminates lead-contaminated waste from the assay process. Samples remain intact and uncontaminated, available for re-assay or further geological analysis.

Based on the 2025 GHD independent lifecycle assessment conducted at SGS Kalgoorlie. View sustainability report

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

PhotonAssay™ uses high-energy X-rays from a linear accelerator to excite atomic nuclei within a sample. The resulting gamma-ray signatures are measured to determine gold and other element concentrations — all without destroying or chemically altering the sample.
Fire assay requires high-temperature furnaces, lead-based fluxes, and hours of processing time. PhotonAssay™ delivers results in approximately two minutes, uses no hazardous chemicals, generates zero Scope 1 emissions, and preserves the sample for re-analysis.
PhotonAssay™ analyses samples up to 500 grams — significantly larger than the typical 30–50 gram fire assay charge. This larger sample size provides more representative results and reduces sampling error.
Yes. PhotonAssay™ units can be deployed directly to mine sites or operated within commercial laboratories. Chrysos offers multiple flexible leasing models to suit different operational requirements.
PhotonAssay™ currently measures gold, silver, and copper concentrations. Research and development is underway to expand the range of detectable elements.
Yes. PhotonAssay™ results have been independently validated and the technology is deployed in accredited commercial laboratories worldwide. A 2025 GHD lifecycle assessment also confirmed its environmental performance benefits.
