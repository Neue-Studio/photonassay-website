Gold usage rules

Gold is used sparingly as an accent — never as a large fill colour. Use it for:
· Eyebrow underlines (60px bar)
· Button bottom border on hover (3px inset)
· Stat numbers
· Quote block left border (3px)
· Badge background (10% opacity)
· Form focus ring (8% opacity)
· Nav underline on hover (2px)
